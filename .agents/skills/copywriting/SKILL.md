# Portfolio Copywriting Skill

Use this framework to turn raw notes, Notion pages, or CV bullets into sharp, evidence-backed case studies and portfolio sections.

## 1. Case Study Architecture (8-Beat Spine)
Give each beat only as much space as the story earns:
1. **Headline & Hard Proof:** `[Strongest Verified Metric in first 6 words] + [What Changed] + [Where]`.
2. **Ugly Before-State & Stakes:** Who was stuck, what manual/broken process they endured, and what it cost ($, time, risk, or drop-off). *Minesh move: open with a 2-line dialogue hook or napkin math.*
3. **Why the Problem Was Hard:** The hidden technical, operational, or market constraint.
4. **Why the Obvious Fix Was Rejected:** What stakeholders asked for, why you said no (*"Right, but wrong"*), and the risk/constraint that forced a different bet.
5. **Product Decision & Intervention:** The specific mechanism or product lever you chose.
6. **Execution, Prioritization & Constraints:** Sequencing, scoping cuts, or hard trade-offs during rollout.
7. **Measurable Outcome, Evidence & Attribution:** Separate output (shipped) → outcome (behavior shift) → business impact. State scope, baseline, period (monthly/annual, pilot/prod, actual/forecast), and personal vs. team attribution.
8. **Reflection:** What you would repeat or change next time.

## 2. Project Card Micro-Copy Formula
- **Eyebrow:** `Company · Role · Year`
- **Title (<= 7 words):** Metric-first outcome title.
- **1-Sentence Hook (<= 18 words):** Problem + Mechanism.
- **3 Metric Pills:** Verified numbers with scope (e.g., `$10M/yr Saved` · `99.99% Uptime` · `Automated Recovery`).

## 3. Final Quality Gate (Must Pass Before Delivery)
Score the draft out of 100 (**Evidence Integrity is a hard gate**—a high score cannot excuse an unsupported claim):
- Business impact (20%)
- Evidence integrity (20% — **Hard Gate**)
- Product thinking & trade-offs (15%)
- Specificity (15%)
- Causal clarity (10%)
- Reader relevance (10%)
- Writing discipline (10%)

**Thresholds:** `90–100` Publish-ready · `80–89` Minor revisions · `70–79` Substantive editing · `<70` Rewrite.

**Pre-Flight Checklist:**
- [ ] Every metric has a verified source, baseline, scope, and time period (no invented numbers; targets/pilots labeled explicitly).
- [ ] Zero process-led openings (*partnered, collaborated, spearheaded*) and zero banned words.
- [ ] Causal chain is clear (problem → mechanism → behavior shift → business outcome) with accurate personal vs. team attribution.
- [ ] Before-state, rejected alternative, and trade-off are explicit.
- [ ] Hero (<25 words) and summaries (<=18 words/sentence) meet length ceilings and pass the `humanizer` 3-pass check and Coffee Test.
