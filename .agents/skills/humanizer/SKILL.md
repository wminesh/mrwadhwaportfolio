# Humanizer Skill (3-Pass Anti-AI Copy Editor)

Use this skill to strip "AI slop" and corporate PM jargon from any text while keeping every hard metric, date, and technical fact intact.

## Pass 1: Structural & Formula Purge (Wikipedia "Signs of AI Writing")
Scan the text and eliminate these structural AI tells:
1. **Contrast Reframes ("Not just X, it's Y")**:
   - *Bad:* "This wasn't just a dashboard; it was a new operating system for the team."
   - *Fix:* State what it actually did in one direct clause.
2. **Participle Tail Clauses (`...ing` endings)**:
   - *Bad:* "...streamlining workflows, improving visibility, and driving adoption across teams."
   - *Fix:* Delete the participle tail completely or replace it with a standalone sentence containing a hard number.
3. **Compulsory Tricolons (Rule-of-3 Addiction)**:
   - *Bad:* "Fast, reliable, and scalable." / "Across engineering, operations, and business stakeholders."
   - *Fix:* Use 1 or 2 specific nouns, or a concrete list of actual artifacts.
4. **Stage-Setting Openings, Hype & Summaries**:
   - *Bad:* "In today's fast-paced retail environment...", "In the ever-evolving landscape...", "Buckle up, because I'm diving into...", or ending with "Ultimately, this project demonstrated..." / "Share your thoughts and let's make the world a better place."
   - *Fix:* Delete the first and last throat-clearing sentences of every section. Start in the middle of the problem or with a blunt 2-line dialogue.
5. **Em-Dash & Colon Crutches**:
   - Limit em-dashes (`—`) to at most 1 per case study. Replace with periods.

## Pass 2: Lexical Purge (Banned Vocabulary)
Immediately replace or delete any occurrence of:
- **AI Verbs:** *delve, underscore, showcase, foster, empower, elevate, spearhead, leverage, utilize, orchestrate, revolutionize, unlock, unleash, navigate.*
- **AI Adjectives:** *seamless, robust, scalable, cutting-edge, holistic, pivotal, transformative, end-to-end, data-driven, user-centric, mission-critical, bespoke, multifaceted.*
- **AI Nouns:** *tapestry, testament, landscape, paradigm, realm, beacon, synergy, ecosystem (unless literal third-party developer ecosystem).*
- **PM Resume Soup:** *cross-functional stakeholders, operational efficiency, workflow optimization, strategic initiatives, execution roadmap.*

## Pass 3: Burstiness, Rhythm & Voice Check
1. **Sentence Length Variance:**
   - Never allow three consecutive sentences of similar length (e.g., three 15-word sentences in a row).
   - Pair a 14–18 word explanatory sentence with a 3–6 word punchline (*"It worked."*, *"Cancellations dropped 40%."*, *"Engineering hated the first draft."*).
2. **Concrete Over Abstract:**
   - Replace every category noun (*"communication channels"*) with the literal tool (*"WhatsApp and Telegram drips"*).
3. **Voice Match (Minesh's Natural Method)**:
   - **Keep:** Dry, candid asides (*"Right, but wrong."*), Socratic questions to the reader, physical metaphors (*leaky bucket*), and literal unit-economics math (`traffic × CVR × AOV`).
   - **Cut:** Polite corporate hedging, 10-point padded FAQ lists, and fake enthusiasm (*"seriously cool stuff"*).
   - **Coffee Test:** Read the output as if speaking across a table to a skeptical VP of Product. Keep contractions (*didn't, we'd, it's*) when natural.
